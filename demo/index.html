Photosensitivity warning

Faraday waves can flash. With Shake up, the pond stands up in a lattice that turns inside out as it oscillates, and the light it focuses onto the bed brightens and darkens parts of the picture with it. At the default Slow Motion (8×) that is slow, about 1.25 times a second at the default 10 Hz Drive. At Slow Motion 1× or 2× it is fast: measured over Resolume's demo clips under its demo bass at full Shake, the picture flashed up to 10 times a second (20 at a 20 Hz Drive), far over the three-a-second line in Ofcom's and WCAG's guidance. Flashing light can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy.

The lattice of stripes, squares or hexagons is also a strong regular pattern across the whole picture, the kind ITU-R BT.1702 and Ofcom's guidance treat as a hazard of their own. This page opens with Shake at 0, where there is no lattice. If anyone watching may be photosensitive, keep Slow Motion at 8× or above.
